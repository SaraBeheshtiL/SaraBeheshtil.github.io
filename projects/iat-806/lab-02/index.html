Lab 02 — Bouncing Smiley Faces

An interactive animation created with p5.js.

This sketch features two bouncing circles that move around the canvas. The first circle grows and shrinks, changes to a random color when it hits the top or bottom wall, and becomes a yellow smiley face when it meets the other smiley face.

Click the canvas to change the first circle's direction. Press the spacebar to pause it and press "R" to resume.
