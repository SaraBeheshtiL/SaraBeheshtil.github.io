Lab 04 — Bohoo & Kiky Dancing Grid

A grid of dancing animations

This sketch shows a dancing grid. This exercise is the extension of what we did in our tutorial.

- When you move the mouse from right to left the grids number goes up. The closer the cell to the mouse, the faster the animation.

- The animations in gray rows, shows Bohoo dancing the Floss and the other rows shows another kind of dancing. I got the frames by Claude.

- While moving the mouse on canvas, the cells' opacity changes by their distance from the mouse. Closer cells go darker and farther cells to the mouse get lighter.

- Also each cell where the mouse is, becomes double in size and shows a new dancing animations. (Bohoo & Kiky)
